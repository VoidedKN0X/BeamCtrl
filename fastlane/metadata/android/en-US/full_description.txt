<p>Turn your phone's infrared (IR) blaster into a TV remote.</p>
<ul>
<li>Code library with 8,500+ remotes, built in and offline</li>
<li>Try every button before you save a remote</li>
<li>Turn off any TV by cycling every TV power code</li>
<li>Ready-made Samsung, LG, Sony, Philips and Vizio remotes, plus Find my TV</li>
<li>Import and export Flipper Zero .ir files, or build a remote button by button</li>
<li>Full TV layout with hold-to-repeat volume, channel and arrow keys</li>
<li>NEC, Samsung, Sony SIRC, RC5/RC6, Pronto and raw codes</li>
<li>Backup and restore all remotes as one file</li>
<li>Light, dark and pure black themes, Material You, tablet layout</li>
</ul>
<p>Needs a phone with a built-in IR blaster.</p>
<p>Google-free and de-Googled: no Google Play services, Firebase or other Google services, and the app never contacts Google. It works the same on de-Googled phones (GrapheneOS, CalyxOS, /e/OS, LineageOS without Google apps).</p>
<p>Telemetry-free: no analytics, crash reporting, usage stats or tracking of any kind. BeamCtrl has no internet permission at all, so it never connects to anything.</p>
